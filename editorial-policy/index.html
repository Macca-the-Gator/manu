Editorial Policy

How Manu prepares, attributes, and updates its content.

Scope

Manu is a personal portfolio and project site. It presents profile information, nursing experience supplied by Emanuele Vieira, visual and technical studies, and links to external references. Blog articles retain their author attribution where shown.

Accuracy and context

Profile and experience details are reviewed by Emanuele Vieira. Educational or technical material is shared for general information and does not replace clinical care or professional advice. Pages are updated when relevant information or project behavior changes.

Sources and attribution

When content relies on external research, documentation, artwork, libraries, or interface references, Manu links or credits the source when practical. Third-party work remains subject to its original license and terms.

Corrections and contact

To report an error, request a correction, or suggest a source, contact emanuele@pklavc.com.
