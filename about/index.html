About Me

Backend Software Engineer focused on applied AI, software architecture, internal systems, automation, and data. I build platforms with Python, FastAPI, Node.js, TypeScript, SQL, and cloud infrastructure to turn real operations into reliable, observable, and evolvable software.

My work combines multi-agent RAG platforms, AI assistants, product backends, automation, data pipelines, and internal systems. I design AI solutions as part of the software itself: with retrievable context, controlled tools, memory, fallback behavior, permissions, logs, and traceability.

I also work with backend architecture, queues, caching, tests, CI/CD, observability, data security, and integrations when they belong to a larger system. The goal is to reduce manual work, clarify operational flows, and keep systems easy to evolve in real usage.

Engineering Operating Model

Signal layer Systems stay readable when their signals stay visible.
System boundaries
Clear contracts between UI, backend, agents, workers, and storage
I keep each responsibility in its own layer so business rules, models, tools, data, and integrations can evolve without invisible coupling.
Replayable execution
Inputs, states, traces, normalized models, and audit trails
AI, automation, and data workflows need to be rerunnable: failures should be inspectable, correctable, and replayable without losing original context.
Operational Feedback
Signals before surprises
Dashboards, logs, real-time events, scheduled checks, and failure summaries help expose drift before it turns into hidden risk.
AI-Assisted Delivery
Agent speed with engineering review
I use AI agents to accelerate exploration, scaffolding, and validation while keeping architecture, security, and production behavior under human review.
